Raincheck Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Published by Scott Berry

The short version
  • Raincheck uses your location only to fetch the weather for where you are.
  • Your location and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold.

What Raincheck uses

  • Your location, if you choose "Use my location". It comes from your device and is used only while the app is open. Raincheck never accesses your location in the background.
  • A city name, if you search for one instead.
  • Your settings: the place you last chose, its address label, temperature units, and the design you picked.

Location and settings are saved in the app's storage on your device so Raincheck opens to your place next time. They stay there until you change them, clear the app's data, or uninstall it.

Who receives information, and why

To show a forecast, Raincheck has to ask weather and map services on the internet. Like any internet request, each service also sees your device's IP address. Raincheck sends the least it can:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Open-Meteo Your position rounded to about 1 km (0.01°), and your unit preference To get the forecast
Open-Meteo geocoding The text you type in city search To find matching places
OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) Your position rounded to about 10 m (0.0001°), only when you have moved more than about 100 m since the last lookup To show the nearest street address under "Forecast location"
GitHub Pages (web version only) Standard web requests for the app's files To deliver the app in a browser

These services handle that information under their own privacy policies, linked above. Raincheck does not send them your name, account details or device identifiers, because it has none of those.

What Raincheck does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data for advertising.
  • No background location access.
  • No servers of its own that store your data.

Permissions

  • Location (only while using the app): to forecast for where you are. You can deny it and search for a city instead.
  • Internet: to download forecasts.

Children

Raincheck is a general-audience weather app. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Forecast accuracy

Forecasts are estimates, not official weather warnings. For severe weather, follow your national weather service.

Changes to this policy

If Raincheck's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
